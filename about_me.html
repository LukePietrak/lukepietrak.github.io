About me

I’m Luke, a student at Ithaca College pursuing a B.S. in Television, Photography, and Digital Media Production (Class of 2028) with minors in Computer Science and Animation & VFX. At my core, I’m a hard-working self-starter wired for both creative media and technical problem-solving. Outside of the edit bay, I’m usually dissecting film, TV, and music. From the raw energy of hip-hop and rap to the atmospheric layers of art rock and alternative rock. I'm constantly learning new skills, from learning to Yo-Yo to playing guitar, or staying active with time outdoors. This mix keeps me sharp, grounded, and constantly pulling new rhythm and ideas into my work.

My approach as a motion designer and video editor is rooted in being self-taught and resilient. Before formalizing my skills in college, I taught myself tools like Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Blender, Maya, and Cinema 4D through countless hours of trial and error from my early teenage years. My wide taste in music and media directly dictates the rhythm, pacing, and mood of my cuts, while my background in computer science gives me a methodical edge when tackling complex pipelines and tight deadlines. I’m not someone who waits around for instructions, when I hit a creative or technical roadblock, I dig in, adapt, and get the job done.
